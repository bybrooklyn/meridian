# Meridian Contributor License Agreement

**Version:** 1.0-draft
**Status:** Draft for counsel review. Not yet in force. Do not accept third-party contributions under this text until a qualified lawyer has reviewed it.
**Governing requirement:** `LEGAL-003` (CLA replaces DCO), with `LEGAL-001` (relicensing envelope), `LEGAL-002` (ownership path), `LEGAL-004` (AI-assisted contributions) and `LEGAL-005` (third-party intake).

## 0. Notice to the reader

This document is a single agreement with two signing paths. Section 9 is for individuals. Section 10 is for entities. Everything in sections 1 through 8 applies to both.

This agreement replaces the Developer Certificate of Origin. It does not supplement it. Once this agreement is in force, `DCO.md` is retired and `Signed-off-by` trailers no longer establish contribution rights.

## 1. Definitions

**"Project"** means Meridian and any other software, documentation or materials maintained by the Project Owner in the repositories the Project Owner designates.

**"Project Owner"** means the individual or entity that owns the Project. As of this version the Project Owner is the individual proprietor of Meridian. The Project Owner may transfer this agreement and all rights granted under it to a successor entity, including Dead Signal Works, LLC, without further consent from You. Section 8.3 governs that transfer.

**"You"** means the individual or entity that agrees to this agreement.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship that You intentionally submit to the Project, in any form and through any medium, including source code, object code, documentation, tests, examples, schemas, configuration, designs, translations, benchmarks, build files, and any accompanying materials. Submission includes pull requests, patches, issue attachments, electronic mailing lists, source control systems, and any other communication channel the Project Owner uses to receive contributions, but excludes any communication conspicuously marked in writing as "Not a Contribution."

**"Submit"** means to send a Contribution to the Project by any of the means described above.

## 2. Copyright ownership stays with You

You retain all right, title and interest in and to Your Contributions, including copyright. This agreement grants a license. It is not an assignment of copyright and it does not transfer ownership of Your Contributions.

You are free to use, license, sell or otherwise exploit Your Contributions for any other purpose, including in other projects and under other licenses.

## 4. Relicensing

You expressly grant the Project Owner the right to license and relicense Your Contributions, and any derivative works of them, under any terms the Project Owner selects, including:

- open-source licenses, including but not limited to MPL-2.0, Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD, GPL-3.0-or-later and LGPL-3.0-or-later;
- source-available terms;
- commercial licenses; and
- proprietary licenses.

The Project Owner may apply different terms to different releases, different distributions, and different recipients, and may offer the same Contribution under more than one license at the same time.

This right is granted so that the Project can change its licensing over its lifetime without tracing and renegotiating with every past contributor. Anyone who has already received a copy of the Project under a given license keeps the rights that license granted them. Relicensing operates on future distributions and does not withdraw rights already granted.

## 5. Patent license grant

This license applies only to those patent claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contributions alone or by combination of Your Contributions with the works to which You Submitted them.

If any entity institutes patent litigation against You or any other entity, including a cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit, alleging that Your Contribution or the work to which You Submitted it constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent license granted to that entity under this agreement for that Contribution terminates as of the date the litigation is filed.

## 6. Your representations

You represent, to the best of Your knowledge, that:

**6.1 Authorship.** Each Contribution is Your original creation, or You have the rights necessary to Submit it and to grant the licenses in this agreement.

**6.2 Authority.** You are legally entitled to grant the licenses in this agreement. If You are an individual whose employer has rights to intellectual property You create, You represent that You have received permission to make the Contribution on behalf of that employer, that Your employer has waived those rights for Your Contributions, or that Your employer has executed a separate entity signing path under section 10.

**6.3 Third-party material.** If any Contribution includes work that is not Your original creation, You will identify it at the time of submission, together with its source, license, author, and any restriction, notice or attribution requirement that attaches to it. You will not include third-party material whose license is incompatible with the licensing envelope in section 4 without first obtaining written agreement from the Project Owner. `LEGAL-005` governs third-party intake and applies in addition to this section.

**6.4 No conflicting obligation.** No Contribution is subject to any agreement, order or obligation that conflicts with the licenses granted here.

## 7. Moral rights

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, You waive, and agree not to assert, any moral rights, rights of attribution, rights of integrity, and any similar rights You may hold in Your Contributions, in favour of the Project Owner and its licensees and sublicensees.

Where such a waiver is not permitted by law, You consent to the Project Owner and its licensees and sublicensees exercising the rights granted in this agreement in a manner that would otherwise infringe those rights, and You agree not to assert those rights against them.

Nothing in this section removes any attribution the Project Owner voluntarily provides, or any attribution required by a third-party license under section 6.3.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 No obligation to use.** The Project Owner is not obliged to accept, merge, use, distribute or maintain any Contribution.

**8.2 No warranty.** Except for the representations in section 6, You provide Your Contributions on an "AS IS" basis, without warranties or conditions of any kind, whether express or implied, including any warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, title, or non-infringement.

**8.3 Assignment.** The Project Owner may assign this agreement and all rights granted under it, in whole or in part, to a successor in interest to the Project, including on incorporation, reorganisation, merger, or sale of the Project's assets. Your grants survive that assignment and run to the assignee on the same terms.

**8.4 Scope over time.** This agreement covers every Contribution You have already Submitted before signing and every Contribution You Submit after signing, until You give the Project Owner written notice that You will make no further Contributions. Such notice is prospective only. It does not withdraw, limit or terminate any grant covering a Contribution already Submitted.

**8.5 Notification of change.** You agree to notify the Project Owner if any representation in section 6 becomes inaccurate.

**8.6 Entire agreement and severability.** This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning Contributions to the Project and supersedes the Developer Certificate of Origin and any prior contribution terms. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions stay in force and the unenforceable provision is limited to the minimum extent necessary.

## 11. Contribution conduct requirements

These requirements sit alongside the licence grants and are conditions of acceptance rather than terms of the licence.

**11.1 AI-assisted contributions.** AI assistance is permitted. Accepted contribution history must not credit AI systems as authors, co-authors, assistants or contributors. This prohibition covers Git author and committer identities, `Co-Authored-By` trailers, `Assisted-By` and `Generated-By` trailers or equivalents, agent-session links, pull request titles and descriptions used as AI credit, source headers and comments, and changelogs, release credits and acknowledgements.

A contribution containing AI-attributed commits is rejected until those commits are removed from the submitted branch history. You rewrite and force-push Your own branch to do so. Ordinary technical discussion of AI products remains permitted.

You remain responsible for reviewing, understanding and validating everything You Submit, and for holding the rights this agreement requires, regardless of how the work was produced. `LEGAL-004` governs this section.

**11.2 Provenance.** Third-party material must arrive with the exact source, revision, hash, SPDX identifier, notices, modifications, owner and any exit or update strategy recorded under the Project's provenance directory before it is used. `LEGAL-005` governs this section.
