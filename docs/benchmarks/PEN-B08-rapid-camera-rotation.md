# PEN-B08 — Rapid Camera Rotation

[Suite](README.md) · [Penumbra](../../MERIDIAN_SPECOMENT.md)

version 1.0 · `DefinitionOnly` · `Uncalibrated`

Purpose: stress visibility churn, Hi-Z/occlusion behavior, streaming prediction,
input-to-frame latency, motion vectors, exposure, temporal rejection, and
disocclusion under a fixed high-angular-velocity camera path. Variants isolate
forest, interior, terrain, transparency, and weather scenes. The recipe fixes
camera curve, scene/corpus hashes, counts, profile, warmup, and cache. Reports
include complete shared fields plus visible-set churn, culling/indirect counts,
history resets/rejection, ghosting/shimmer captures, and worst frame windows.
