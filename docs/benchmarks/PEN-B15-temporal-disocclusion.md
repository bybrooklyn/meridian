# PEN-B15 — Temporal Disocclusion

[Suite](README.md) · [Penumbra](../../MERIDIAN_SPECOMENT.md)

version 1.0 · `DefinitionOnly` · `Uncalibrated`

Purpose: produce controlled reveal/occlusion, thin foliage, fast motion, camera
cuts, exposure changes, origin rebases, dynamic-resolution changes, transparency,
and weather to validate history lifetime. The recipe fixes scene/path/events,
ground-truth/reference captures where possible, hashes, profile, warmup, and
cache. Reports include complete shared fields plus motion/history validity,
rejection/reset counts, ghosting/shimmer/trailing, exposure response, debug
overlays, and human perceptual review. No single image metric decides quality.
