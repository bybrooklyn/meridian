# PEN-B01 — Midnight Forest

[Suite](README.md) · [Validation](../../MERIDIAN_SPECOMENT.md) · [Prototype](../../MERIDIAN_SPECOMENT.md)

version 1.0 · Status `DefinitionOnly` · Calibration `Uncalibrated`

## Purpose

Measure Penumbra's representative night forest: dense trees and ground cover,
flashlight/local lighting, alpha-tested foliage, cascaded and local shadows,
fog/readability, terrain/vegetation streaming, rapid and normal camera motion,
temporal stability, memory, and frame pacing.

The executable corpus will be generated/redacted. A private Project Meridian
checkpoint may contribute only an immutable authority/corpus hash and semantic
expectations; no route, document, logo, narrative text, or proprietary asset is
stored here.

## Recipe contract

The future sanitized Alluvium recipe fixes recipe/version/provenance hashes,
determinism level, evaluation mode, seed, source/build hashes, object/material/light counts,
cell layout, weather state, camera/input path, capture markers, quality profile,
required capabilities, warmup, cache state, and expected state transitions.
Cold, warm, rapid-rotation, traversal, memory-pressure, and recovery variants
must isolate one dimension at a time.

## Required evidence

Use the complete Penumbra report contract in
``workloads.json``, including CPU/GPU/frame
distributions and lows, stalls, memory, churn, overdraw, shadow/volumetric cost,
temporal stability, captures/artifacts, missing features, and raw evidence.

No numeric acceptance threshold exists until the executable corpus is calibrated
on named capability profiles. Occluded/structural smoke cannot satisfy visible
quality.
