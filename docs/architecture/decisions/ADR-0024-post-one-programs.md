## Context

Complete official framework families, advanced cinematic character tools, native VCS storage, Collective ecosystem expansion, MMO/distributed-world infrastructure, advanced integrity, professional modeling, Marquee, and competitive performance/quality leadership are important but cannot honestly become implicit blockers for Project Meridian or Meridian Engine 1.0.

## Decision

`MS-00` through `MS-10` remain the complete Meridian Engine 1.0 evidence roadmap. Long-horizon, separately releasable capabilities use `PRG-<DOMAIN>-NNN` program identifiers. A program records architecture, dependencies, entry evidence, non-goals, security/operations obligations, release criteria, and candidate work packages without pretending implementation readiness.

Programs may perform architecture-preserving research before 1.0. They may not complete an MS milestone, promote subsystem maturity, or add mandatory minimal-profile dependencies. Activation requires a future planning review and bounded `WP-*` packages.

## Consequences

- The roadmap does not grow an unbounded sequence of speculative milestones.
- Strategic work remains visible and testable without false scheduling precision.
- Funding- and operations-dependent services remain honest.
- Program completion and Meridian Engine release qualification are separate evidence events.
