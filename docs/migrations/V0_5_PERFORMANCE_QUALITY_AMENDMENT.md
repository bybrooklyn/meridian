# v0.5 Competitive Performance and Environmental Quality Amendment

Date: 2026-07-16

This ledger records the user-authorized v0.5 amendment that preserves Meridian's
goal of material performance and quality leadership without creating a false
current claim or extending the MS-00 through MS-10 critical path.

## Decision mapping

| Amendment subject | Winning authority | Disposition |

## Status and implementation boundary

The amendment changes specifications, ADRs, registries, validation contracts,
and future planning only. It implements no renderer path, weather field, fluid,
fire, smoke, thermal solver, cost predictor, comparator adapter, benchmark
corpus, or superiority evidence. Isobar and vegetation remain scaffolded;
Torsant and the competitive program have no implementation crate or active
package.
